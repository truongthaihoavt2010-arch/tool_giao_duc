# Project Rules

## Document Formatting
- **Vietnamese Administrative Documents (Nghị định 30/2020/NĐ-CP):** Whenever generating or formatting `.docx` files for Vietnamese administrative or educational purposes (like Báo cáo, Sáng kiến kinh nghiệm, Tờ trình), you MUST comply with Decree 30/2020/NĐ-CP standards:
  - Paper size: A4 (210mm x 297mm).
  - Margins: Top 20-25mm, Bottom 20-25mm, Left 30-35mm, Right 15-20mm.
  - Font: Times New Roman, Size 14.
  - Paragraph formatting: Justified alignment (Căn đều hai bên), line spacing 1.15 to 1.5, space before/after 6pt.
  - First-line indent: 1.27cm.
  - Titles must be centered, uppercase, and bold where required by the decree.
